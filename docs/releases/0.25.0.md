# DEDAL Core 0.25.0 — Creative Production Wave

Date: 2026-09-14

## Why this release exists

Ordinary Creator work exposed two concrete capability gaps after the previous expansion wave closed: professional video production and professional presentation production. Both are recurring workflows with substantial manual effort and clear ownership not covered by existing faculties.

## Added

### Video Production

New top-level faculty for temporal media production:
- generative-video shot direction and take evaluation;
- scene/shot coverage and temporal continuity;
- editing, reframing, retiming and transcoding decisions;
- subtitles/captions and audio finishing;
- motion-graphics/programmatic-video orchestration;
- final media verification and delivery.

Progressive references distinguish provider-neutral generative-video direction from FFmpeg/Remotion-style post-production.

### Presentation Engineering

New top-level faculty for professional deck production:
- audience/objective and narrative framing;
- slide-role architecture and evidence mapping;
- chart/diagram/visual hierarchy decisions;
- speaker notes and template fidelity;
- editable PPTX/source-first strategy;
- rendered slide QA and delivery readiness.

## Boundary decisions

- Visual Direction owns still-image/reference continuity; Video Production owns temporal assembly and motion direction.
- Writing/Editorial owns prose; Video Production owns timing/performance/edit context and Presentation Engineering owns slide communication architecture.
- Research/domain owners remain factual authorities for CME, scientific, annual, or other evidence-bearing decks.
- FFmpeg, Remotion, Veo/Flow, Runway, Seedance, native slide tools, PptxGenJS and similar systems are execution/reference surfaces, not permanent faculty identities.
- Encode success does not prove video editorial quality; PPTX validity does not prove rendered slide quality.
