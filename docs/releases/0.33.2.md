# DEDAL Core 0.33.2 — Editing-Mode Refinement

_Date: 2026-09-19_

## Purpose

Refine the existing Video Production faculty using evidence from hands-on Creator editing work without adding another top-level skill.

## Added

- Two explicit footage-editing methods under Video Production:
  - **Simple / Narrative Edit** for source-meaning, chronology, dialogue/action continuity, and compact scene preservation.
  - **Fan / Visual-Montage Edit** for subject/motif-focused, music-led, visually selective edits where the source scene is raw material rather than the final product.
- Progressive reference `skills/video-production/references/editing-modes.md` covering mode selection, rough-cut acceptance, subject-dominant shot selection, crop-through-motion review, transition discipline, and intentional endings.
- Creative-production regression cases that distinguish the two methods and reject effect-first finalization or default flash-on-every-cut behavior.

## Changed

- Video Production now explicitly separates structural rough acceptance from final effects polish.
- Target-centric fan edits are instructed to minimize secondary-character coverage unless it serves the edit, verify moving-shot framing rather than a single still, and close intentionally on the target motif when appropriate.
- Repeated full-frame white flashes/strobes are explicitly non-default because high-salience transitions can overwhelm the content and reduce viewer comfort.

## Evidence / limits

- The trigger was a real iterative fan-edit workflow in which Creator feedback improved shot-value selection, chronology, secondary-character coverage, subject framing, ending choice, and transition taste.
- Public Core stores only portable editing rules. Creator/channel-specific preferences, exact scene decisions, environment-specific renderer limits, and publishing outcomes remain in the private operational overlay.
- This refinement does not claim that one fan-edit style is universally superior or that any fixed Short duration is optimal.
