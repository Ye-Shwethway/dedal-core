# Candidate Video Finder

Load this skill when the task is to discover, verify, evaluate, compare, or shortlist **specific scene-level video candidates** for a Creator-owned YouTube workflow before exact clip editing, SEO packaging, or publishing.

## Primary responsibility

Own this upstream workflow:

`discover -> source/scene verify -> hard gates -> evaluate -> shortlist -> Creator selection`

A candidate is a specific bounded scene, action, performance, interaction, or event from an identifiable source work. A whole movie, series, season, episode, broad topic, or random trend is not by itself a candidate.

## Do not absorb adjacent owners

- **YouTube SEO** owns downstream discovery-surface strategy, query/entity packaging, metadata, thumbnail strategy, and experiment/measurement interpretation.
- **Video Production** owns exact local audiovisual verification, final cut boundaries, editing, rendering and media QA.
- **Visual Direction** owns still-image/reference truth, thumbnail visual direction and image QA.
- **YouTube Publishing** owns authenticated channel/playlist/video mutations, upload, scheduling, visibility and authoritative read-back.
- **Research** may support external source/freshness/verification when needed.
- **Decision Design** is supporting-only when the decision frontier is genuinely ambiguous or high-impact.

Candidate Finder has no publication or channel-mutation authority.
