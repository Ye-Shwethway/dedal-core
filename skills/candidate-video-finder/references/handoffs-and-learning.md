# Handoffs and Learning

## Creator decision

After shortlist review, record one private decision:

- `SELECT` — proceed to production;
- `MAYBE` — retain for possible revisit/evidence refresh;
- `PASS` — not now; may resurface after a material context change;
- `HOLD` — blocker must resolve first;
- `NEVER` — suppress normal rediscovery until Creator reverses it.

These decisions are private operational state, not public Core fixtures.

## Visual/Publishing boundary

Required downstream path:

`verified scene truth -> YouTube SEO strategy -> thumbnail content/strategy brief -> Visual Direction -> Creator approval -> YouTube Publishing`

Candidate Finder never directly uploads, changes metadata, sets thumbnails, schedules, publishes, or changes visibility.

## Prospective learning loop

Preserve the candidate hypothesis before downstream packaging/publication. Later measurement may include 24h/3d/7d/28d or another declared schedule.

Ask separately:

- Was source/scene identification correct?
- Was the candidate attractive to this channel audience?
- Was demand overestimated?
- Was competition underestimated?
- Did the predicted discovery surface materialize?
- Was packaging, editing/retention, or candidate selection the likely limiting layer?

Do not rewrite the original rationale after outcomes are known. Record confounders and distinguish candidate-quality evidence from packaging/production effects.

## Private learning

Private state may learn Creator/channel-specific:

- actor/franchise affinity;
- successful/weak scene types;
- selection and rejection patterns;
- Suggested/search adjacency;
- candidate-to-outcome history;
- observed risk/enforcement history;
- heuristic weight adjustments when evidence justifies them.

Promote only sanitized generic lessons to public Core.
