## ADAPT
1. Triage reusable candidates as discard/improve/merge/create before durable mutation.
2. Separate captured memory from learned mastery with explicit maturity states.
3. Require recurrence, held-out, or transfer evidence before broad promotion when severity does not justify a one-shot invariant.
4. Allow lightweight automatic capture, but keep promotion evidence-gated and authority-bounded.
5. Use production traces/corrections to seed targeted and regression evals.

## ALREADY COVERED
Canonical-source reconciliation, source-sync debt, protected evals, regression guards, public/private routing, authority boundaries, revert/inconclusive decisions, and live verification.

## REJECT
Unbounded self-rewrite, automatic one-shot promotion, third-party hook/runtime installation by default, imported storage conventions, popularity-as-proof, and evaluator weakening.

## Decision
TUNE the existing Self-Improvement skill; do not replace it and do not create another overlapping faculty. Evidence level after this audit is multi-source external-method research plus existing real-regression/contract evidence; long-run recurrence reduction remains unmeasured.
