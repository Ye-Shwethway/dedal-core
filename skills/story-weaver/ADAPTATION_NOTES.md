# Adaptation Notes — Story Weaver

_Date: 2026-09-30_

Story Weaver is a DEDAL-native narrative faculty created to keep fictional canon/scene reasoning distinct from downstream image/video production.

## Adapted principles
- scene purpose and state change before decorative prose;
- character goals/knowledge/constraints as causal drivers;
- beat/reversal/action-reaction structure without requiring a rigid formula;
- emotional continuity and setup/payoff tracking;
- explicit environment/prop/time facts when they constrain later visualization;
- structured visual handoff that supplies story truth but leaves shot/camera authority to Visual Narrative Production.

## Boundary choices
- line editing/translation stays with Writing/Editorial;
- still-image shot design/generation stays with Visual Narrative Production;
- temporal assembly/motion direction stays with Video Production;
- factual claims use Research/domain authority;
- private story canon stays in project/private state, not public Core.
