# Character State and Emotional Continuity

Track only story-relevant state:
- current goals and priorities;
- knowledge/beliefs and misconceptions;
- relationship state;
- injuries/fatigue/resources;
- promises/secrets/obligations;
- current emotional posture;
- what just happened that affects behavior.

Emotional change should normally have a visible or inferable cause. Distinguish outward performance from internal state: a character can appear calm while internally afraid, or act angry to hide grief.

When scenes are separated by time or location, explicitly decide what emotional/physical state carries forward and what plausibly changes off-screen.
