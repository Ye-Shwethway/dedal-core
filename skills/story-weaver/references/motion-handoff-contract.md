# Motion Handoff Contract

When accepted story truth must become generated motion, Story Weaver supplies **narrative state**, not provider syntax.

Include when material:

- scene/beat ID and narrative function;
- characters and current internal/outward state;
- observable action and causal order;
- start state and required end state;
- relationship/emotional change that must read on screen;
- critical props/environment and ownership/position facts;
- information that must remain hidden/ambiguous;
- continuity constraints inherited from adjacent beats.

Camera choreography, motion-reference strategy, provider selection, prompt syntax, and take QA belong downstream to Generative Video Direction (with Visual Narrative Production supplying visual anchors when needed).
