# Visual Handoff Contract

When handing a scene to Visual Narrative Production, provide story truth, not a finished image prompt.

Include when material:
- scene ID / location / time;
- selected narrative beat(s);
- characters present and current visible state;
- relationship/emotional state;
- observable action/blocking required by canon;
- story-critical props and ownership/position facts;
- environment facts that must be visible or preserved;
- what changed from the previous scene/beat;
- what must remain ambiguous or unrevealed;
- any iconic or continuity-critical detail.

Do not prescribe lens, camera angle, lighting recipe, or image-model syntax unless those are themselves canonical story facts. Those choices belong downstream.

For a newly authored scene, apply `causal-scene-design.md` first. Include beat ID, trigger/choice/consequence, character-versus-audience knowledge, reveal locks, resource/hand constraints, and labeled provisional staging when material. Downstream physical staging may refine implementation but must not silently alter narrative facts.
